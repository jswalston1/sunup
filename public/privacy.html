Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

What SunUp collects on this site: nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies set by SunUp, and no personal information collected, stored, or sold.

Use my location: If you tap "Use my location," your device's location is used only in your browser to pick the nearest market (Utah, Memphis, or West Palm Beach). It is never sent to or stored by SunUp.

What the infrastructure sees: Cloudflare, the service that hosts and protects this site, processes standard web-request data (IP address, browser, pages requested) to serve the site and protect it from abuse. SunUp does not use that data to identify individuals.

Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics only — cookieless, no personal data. It counts page views and measures load performance; it does not identify you.

Outbound booking links: Clicking a booking button takes you to a third-party booking site (such as Chronogolf, foreUP, TenFore, MemberSports, or the course's own site). Those sites have their own privacy policies. SunUp does not control what they collect and receives no data back from them.

No cookies: This site sets no cookies. If Cloudflare's bot protection ever sets a short-lived security cookie to distinguish real visitors from bots, it is used only for that purpose.

Contact: Questions about this policy? Email Jwalston84@gmail.com.

Changes: Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
