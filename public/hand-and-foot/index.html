How scoring works
  • Each round, your score is the points from your melds and canastas, minus the cards left in your hand and foot when the round ends. Scores can be negative — enter them with a minus sign.
  • Card values: jokers 50, aces and 2s 20, kings through 8s 10, 7s through 4s and black 3s 5.
  • Canastas: clean (no wild cards) 500, dirty (with wild cards) 300.
  • Red 3s are bonus cards — agree their value before the first round (commonly 500).
  • Some tables add a bonus for going out — agree it before you start.

House rules vary from table to table. The numbers above are the usual ones; agree any differences before the first deal.
